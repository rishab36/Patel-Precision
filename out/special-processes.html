Special Processes

Specialized Manufacturing

Advanced processes for complex manufacturing requirements

Beyond Machining

We offer a full range of secondary and special processes — either in-house or through our trusted network of specialized partners — to deliver fully finished, ready-to-use components.

Heat Treatment

Hardening, tempering, annealing, case hardening, and stress relieving to achieve required mechanical properties and surface hardness for precision components.

  • Through Hardening
  • Case Hardening (Carburizing)
  • Induction Hardening
  • Stress Relieving
  • Annealing & Normalizing

Surface Coating & Plating

Electroplating and surface coating processes to enhance corrosion resistance, wear resistance, and aesthetic appearance of machined components.

  • Zinc Plating (Clear & Yellow)
  • Nickel Plating
  • Chrome Plating
  • Phosphating
  • Anodizing (for Aluminium)

Surface Finishing

Precision surface finishing processes to achieve required surface roughness values and dimensional accuracy for tight-tolerance applications.

  • Cylindrical Grinding (ID & OD)
  • Surface Grinding
  • Lapping & Honing
  • Polishing & Buffing
  • Deburring & Edge Breaking

Assembly & Sub-Assembly

In-house assembly capabilities for multi-component assemblies including pressing, fastening, testing, and packaging.

  • Press-fit Assembly
  • Threaded Assembly
  • Valve Assembly
  • Functional Testing
  • Custom Packaging

EDM & Wire Cutting

Electrical Discharge Machining for complex geometries and hard materials that cannot be machined using conventional methods.

  • Wire EDM
  • Sinker EDM
  • Micro EDM
  • Complex Profile Cutting

Testing & Certification

Comprehensive material and dimensional testing services through NABL-certified laboratories for quality certification.

  • Material Composition Testing
  • Tensile & Hardness Testing
  • Dimensional Inspection Reports
  • NABL-Certified Lab Testing
  • Customer-Specific Test Certificates
