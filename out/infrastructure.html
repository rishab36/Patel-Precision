Our Facility

World-Class Manufacturing Facility

Patel Precision Pvt. Ltd. has its office and manufacturing facility located in Thane, near Mumbai. The factory has a total operational area of 10,000 sq. ft. with an additional 3,000 sq. ft. expansion space available. The factory works in shifts 24×7.

We have all standard instruments for 2D inspection like Trimos, Digital Height Gauges, Digital Micrometers, Digital Verniers, and Dials. We also have a tie-up with NABL certified LAB for Raw Material testing and for 3D inspection we have Mitutoyo CMM, Vision system & Surface tester near our company.

All this, coupled with great material handling and storing facility, skilled labour, dedicated team leaders and our experience in the industry make us an ideal choice for all machining requirements.
