Who We Are

Precision Engineering

Since 1999

Established in 1999, Patel Precision Pvt Ltd has been at the forefront of precision machining and CNC manufacturing in India. Our state-of-the-art facility in Bhiwandi, Maharashtra is equipped with advanced CNC machines including multi-axis turning centers, VMC machines, and 5-axis machining centers.

With over 25 years of experience, we have developed expertise across diverse industries including automotive, aerospace, medical devices, oil & gas, and general engineering.

We serve clients in over 15 countries worldwide, providing precision components that meet the most exacting international standards with tolerances as tight as ±0.005mm.
