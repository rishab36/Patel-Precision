About Us

Patel Precision Pvt Ltd is an ISO 9001:2015 certified CNC Machining shop in Thane near Mumbai. Proudly serving a wide range of industries, domestic and international with the highest quality of products like

  • 5 axis CNC machined components: We manufacture many critical components on our 5axis VMC machines like Load-cell body for weighing scales, Gear box housings, Aerospace components & Defence Components.
  • Precision Machined Components :We manufacture Precision CNC Machined components like Centring Plates, Module parts for weighing scales, Dish washer components, Flanges, Shafts, Adaptors, couplings, Automotive Gear Blanks, Clutch and Brake parts.
  • Air Compressor Valves: We manufacture Air compressor valve assemblies for replacement market to the specification of ELGI 100 dia, ELGI 60 dia, Kirloskar Cr105, 90Ds, 100Ds etc. We also manufacture Valve Plates as per customers requirements.
  • Spray Nozzles: We manufacture all type of Spray nozzles like Jet spray nozzles, Full Cone spray nozzles, and Tank Cleaning spray nozzles as per customers requirements.
  • CNC Machining Services: We also undertake precision CNC Job work like Automotive Gears & Housings, CNC Turned components, 5axis VMC machined components and many critical machined assemblies.

Our wide range of capabilities such as our CNC Machining Cell which includes 5 Axis VMC machines, 4 Axis Vmc machines, CNC turning Lathes, Bandsaw machines and Cylindrical & surface Grinding machines. Patel precision can take care of all your machining needs, from design and development to final product.

We do machining on most of the materials like steel and its alloys, Aluminium, Brass, All Stainless Steels( 1.4301, 1.4304, 1.4305, 1.4401,1.4404, 1.4057, 1.4542, Duplex Steel etc) , Castings & Forgings, and customer specific material. We are a pioneer in machining high precision components & sub-Assemblies. We cater to wide range of industries like Engineering, Automobile, Defence & Aerospace. We strive for excellence in every field and thus consistently work towards achieving total customer satisfaction by upgrading our technology skills to meet the ever rising demands of quality, cost and timely delivery. We are in this business for almost 23 years and serving wide range of industries with its customers spread all across the globe. Our Machines are capable of machining close tolerances & CpK over 1.6 and all critical manufacturing processes are done in house. We always strive to manufacture Zero Defect Quality products at a very competitive cost
